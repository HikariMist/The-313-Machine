## AI-assisted development disclosure (for Steam's content survey)
- **Pre-generated content:** game code, story/dialogue text, placeholder art, music and sound effects were produced
  with AI assistance (Claude by Anthropic) under the studio's direction; key art and character designs were produced
  by the studio with AI image generation. All content is reviewed by the studio before release.
- **Live-generated content:** none. The game does not call any AI service at runtime and works offline.

## Title check (preliminary, not legal advice)
A web search on 2026-10-21 found no existing game titled "The 3:13 Machine". Other vending-machine horror games exist
(e.g. *Vended*, *The Vending Machine*). A trademark search (USPTO/EUIPO) is recommended before launch.
