# Steam long description

**Some customers shouldn't exist. Unfortunately, they still expect service.**

Meridian Roadside Services has hired you to run Unit 313, a lonely vending machine at a rest stop on Route 9. The job is simple: stock the machine, serve the customers, follow the handbook, and survive until 6:00 AM.

Then the clock reaches 3:13.

A man arrives soaked to the bone on a dry night, asking for water. A pleasant woman keeps asking whether you've seen her daughter, and her smile is a little wider every time. A passenger waits for a bus that stopped running decades ago. A tall stranger in a hat would very much like to buy whatever the machine makes on its own.

**Read the rules. Then decide which ones to trust.**

- **Seven night shifts.** A complete psychological horror campaign with more than 50 handcrafted encounters.
- **Observation is survival.** Inspect faces, hands and reflections. Check the security cameras: who appears on CAM 2, and who doesn't?
- **A handbook that changes.** New pages appear in the middle of your shift. Some carry the District seal. Some don't.
- **A working vending machine.** Order stock, load the slots, punch in codes, refuse service, reset jammed motors, and read every receipt.
- **15 endings.** Escape, become the machine, or uncover the truth about Coach 313. Every ending has its own achievement.
- **The machine remembers.** Finishing the story starts a new cycle, and the customers remember you.
- **Endless Mode.** Discover all 15 endings to unlock an endless shift where the rules stay the same and the customers don't.
- **Fair horror.** Every deadly mistake can be avoided with the information you have. Scares escalate slowly from quiet wrongness to full dread, with options to reduce sudden audio, flashing and shake.
- **An original soundtrack** of 15 dark ambient pieces that shift with the night.

No internet connection required.
