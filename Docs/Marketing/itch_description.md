# itch.io description

**THE 3:13 MACHINE** — a psychological horror game about the worst night job on Route 9.

You work the night shift at a roadside vending machine. Most customers are tired truckers and lost students. But at **3:13 AM** the boundary thins, and the ones who come to the glass are not always alive, and not always what they look like.

Follow the employee handbook. Check the cameras. Watch the reflections. Serve them, refuse them, or simply wait for them to leave. Your choices across seven nights decide which of **15 endings** you reach.

- 7-night story campaign, 50+ encounters
- Handbook rules, security cameras, maintenance panel, and a working vending machine
- 15 endings with achievements, a looping cycle structure, and an unlockable Endless Mode
- Original soundtrack, accessibility and comfort options
- Windows installer or portable download · offline play

A Hikari Forge Studios game.
