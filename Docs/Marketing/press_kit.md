# Press kit — THE 3:13 MACHINE

- **Developer / Publisher:** Hikari Forge Studios
- **Platforms:** Windows (Steam, itch.io)
- **Price:** $4.99 USD
- **Genre:** Psychological horror / mystery / management
- **Length:** 2–3 hours for a first playthrough (to be confirmed by playtesting); 15 endings; Endless Mode
- **Elevator pitch:** You run a roadside vending machine on the night shift. At 3:13 AM, customers arrive who shouldn't exist, and you have to decide who to serve.

## Description
Meridian Roadside Services needs a night attendant for Unit 313. The pay is fine and the rules are strange. After 3:13 AM the dead come back to the last stop on Route 9: the driver of a bus that went into the reservoir in 1987, a passenger still waiting for it, a little girl in a yellow raincoat, and things that have learned to wear their faces. Inspect every customer, check the cameras, read the handbook, and decide who gets served.

## Features
- Seven-night campaign, 50+ encounters, 15 endings with achievements
- Observation-driven horror: reflections, cameras, receipts, a self-rewriting handbook
- Cycles: finishing the game starts a new night shift that remembers you
- Unlockable Endless Mode
- Original soundtrack; comfort options for scares, flashing and shake
