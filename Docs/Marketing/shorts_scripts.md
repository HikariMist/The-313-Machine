# Short-form video scripts (TikTok / YouTube Shorts, 20–30 s, vertical 1080×1920)

All footage must be captured from the real game.

## 1 — "Would you serve him?"
- 0–3s: Black. Red LCD flicks on: 3:13 AM. Text: "You work the night shift at a vending machine."
- 3–10s: The Drenched Man at the glass. Caption: "He's soaking wet. It hasn't rained in weeks."
- 10–16s: Cursor clicks REFLECTION. "His reflection drips in time with him."
- 16–22s: Night 3, the same man again. Reflection doesn't move. Caption: "Same face. Would you serve him?"
- 22–26s: Hard cut to black, stinger. Text: "THE 3:13 MACHINE — wishlist now."

## 2 — "The handbook is lying"
- 0–4s: Handbook page, District seal. Rule B1 highlighted.
- 4–10s: Paper rustle; a new page appears with a purple "REV. 3:13" stamp.
- 10–18s: Green handwriting fades in: "There's no seal. This isn't from Hale."
- 18–25s: Caption: "Some rules keep you alive. Some don't. 15 endings." Logo.

## 3 — "Check the cameras"
- 0–5s: A polite woman at the glass: "Just a little chat, dear?"
- 5–12s: Player opens CAM 3 (stockroom). The same woman is inside, smiling at the camera.
- 12–20s: Caption: "Rule A3: if they're in two places at once, don't answer the one in front of you." Player waits, motionless; she fades.
- 20–26s: Logo, "THE 3:13 MACHINE."
