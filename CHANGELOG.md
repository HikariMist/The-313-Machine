# Changelog

## 0.9.0 — first playable (unreleased)
- Seven-night campaign at the Unit 313 vending machine, 52 handcrafted encounters.
- Handbook with sealed and forged pages, security cameras (CAM 0–4), maintenance panel, override keypad.
- 15 endings, each with an achievement; the campaign loops into new cycles that remember earlier ones.
- Endless Mode, unlocked by discovering all 15 endings.
- Six upgrades, inventory and supplier, composure (instability) system, hallucinations, progressive scares.
- 15 original music tracks, 74 sound effects, comfort settings (scare intensity, flashing, shake).
- Dedicated Windows launcher and installer.
